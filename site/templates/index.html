Lightweight by design

CashflowPot does not replace Primavera P6, MS Project, ERP systems, accounting software or detailed project cost control.

It is designed for situations where a credible cash-flow forecast is needed without maintaining a fully cost-loaded programme, or where the current programme is not sufficiently updated for the decision at hand.
